Privacy policy

Effective date: September 09, 2024

FloZ Studio ("us", "we", or "our") operates the https://floz.tech/ website (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal data when you visit and use our Service, and your rights in relation to that data.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Personal Data

You may provide us with certain personal data, e.g.:

  • Name, email address
  • Company name, position
  • Other contact data submitted by you

1.2 Usage data

We automatically collect usage data when you use our Service, including:

  • IP address, browser type and version
  • Pages you visited, timestamps, duration
  • Device identifiers, operating system version
  • Clicks, interactions, navigation paths

1.3 Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, tags, scripts, and similar tracking technologies to track activity and collect information:

  • Session cookies
  • Preference cookies
  • Security cookies

You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to notify you — but this may limit your use of some features.

2. How we use data

We use collected data for purposes such as:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining the Service
  • Improving and personalizing the Service
  • Communicating with you (notifications, support)
  • Analytics and monitoring usage
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, or technical issues

Our processing is based on lawful grounds, such as:

  • Your consent (e.g. for cookies, newsletter)
  • Performance of a contract (e.g. delivering services)
  • Legitimate interests (e.g. improving UX, analytics)

3. Data transfer

Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries different from your own, including the United States. We will ensure adequate protections (e.g. standard contractual clauses, data protection agreements) to safeguard your data.

4. Disclosure of data

We may disclose your Personal Data if required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety. We also may share data with third-party service providers who perform functions on our behalf (analytics, hosting, tools), under confidentiality obligations.

5. Data security

We implement security measures (e.g. encryption, access controls, monitoring) to protect your personal data. Still, no system is completely foolproof, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. We will retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct or update it
  • Delete it (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Port your data (data portability)
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)

To exercise these rights, contact us at floz.tech.official@gmail.com.

We will respond within applicable statutory timeframes.

7. Cookies

We provide more detailed cookie-notice and allow you to control which categories of cookies are used (strictly necessary, preferences, analytics, marketing).

8. Children

Our Service is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we discover such data was submitted, we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes (e.g. via email or prominent notice) and update the “Effective date” at the top.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at: floz.tech.official@gmail.com
