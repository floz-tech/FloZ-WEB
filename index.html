FAQs About Working With FloZ

How does project pricing work at FloZ?

We price based on project scope, complexity, and specific deliverables. We offer clear, fixed-price project estimates or milestone-based phases with no surprise costs.

How are project timelines determined?

Timelines are established during discovery based on feature requirements. We work in iterative sprints, delivering regular progress demos at every stage.

Can you improve an existing website or application?

Yes. We can redesign interfaces, optimize slow performance, modernize legacy codebases, or develop new features seamlessly onto your current stack.

Do I need a technical plan before contacting FloZ?

No technical plan is needed. You only need an idea or a problem you want solved. We will help shape the technical roadmap and architecture with you.

How do you handle communication and project updates?

We communicate directly via Discord, email, or Slack. You get direct access to the developers building your project, with prompt replies and regular updates.
