The challenge

This is not the first time that Nurse CE Club has reached out to us for a website revamp. The very first time we worked on the client's website was in far 2020 resulting in the creation of various graphic elements.

After 4 years, the client got ready for a complete redesign of the website, including rethinking the website structure, developing a new style and creating unique 3D characters. Additionally, it was required to come up with and create illustrations for all course categories available in the product.

Product overview

Product overview

Nurse CE Club offers high quality online continuing education to enhance nursing practice and improve patient care. The company is accredited nationally and approved by the American Nurses Credentialing Centre's Commission on Accreditation (ANCC).

Nurse CE Club has over 200 contact hours across a wide variety of relevant nursing professional development topics.

A set of 3D illustrations was developed for the site, supporting the overall style of the brand
Redesigned the structure of the homepage and prioritised the blocks, increasing the conversion rate of online course subscriptions
We pay special attention to the mobile version and check the site on various devices
